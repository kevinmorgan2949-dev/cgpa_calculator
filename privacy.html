Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

We do not collect, store on our servers, or share any personal information. The app has no accounts, no sign-up, and no analytics or tracking.

The courses, units and grades you enter are saved only on your own phone, so they are still there when you reopen the app. This data never leaves your device. You can delete it at any time using the "Clear all" button or by clearing the app's storage in your phone settings.

We do not share any data with third parties.
